NOVA GENTLEMEN — Style Chart Edition

A bold, light-toned alternate direction for the same barbershop concept, built around a numbered “style chart” you point at to book.

Project overview

This is a second, distinctly different design direction for the NOVA GENTLEMEN barbershop concept. Where the first version is dark and editorial, this one is light, loud and poster-like, with oversized condensed type in navy and a red accent.

Its central idea is the style chart: eight numbered haircut styles, like the board on a barbing-salon wall. Visitors point at a number and it carries through into a six-step booking that ends in a ticket-style summary sent on WhatsApp. It also adds a price list and a “house rules” section.

This is a self-initiated portfolio project, not a paid client engagement. Business names, prices, reviews and other details shown inside it are sample content created for presentation.

The story

Why it looks and works this way.

The brief

Same barbershop, different question: what if booking started the way it does in a real barbing salon, by pointing at a style on the wall? We explored that idea as a separate design direction.

How we approached it

  1. 01

    Borrow a familiar object

    Eight numbered cuts, laid out like the style board in a barbing salon. Visitors point at a number instead of trying to describe a haircut.

  2. 02

    Let the number travel

    The chosen style carries through into a six-step booking: service, style number, chair, day, time and name.

  3. 03

    End with a ticket

    A ticket-style summary shows the whole booking, including the price to pay in the studio, before it is sent on WhatsApp.

  4. 04

    Say it loudly

    Oversized condensed lettering in navy and red, a price list and a short set of house rules give the studio a confident, poster-like voice.

Where it stands

A finished, live alternate direction that sits beside the first version. This is a self-initiated project, so there are no client results to report and we don’t claim any. What it shows is how we think and build.

What’s inside

  • Numbered style chart of eight cuts that feeds straight into booking
  • Price list with durations in Naira
  • House-rules section that communicates the studio’s standards
  • Barber “chair” profiles with a book-this-chair action
  • Six-step booking (service, style number, chair, day, time, name) with a ticket-style summary sent via WhatsApp
  • Time-aware slots and an open-now indicator
  • Opening hours, address and directions section
