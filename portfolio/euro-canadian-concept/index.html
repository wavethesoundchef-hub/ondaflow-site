Project overview

Euro Canadian Concept is a concept website for a design-build construction company in Vancouver. The brand idea is “from blueprint to built”, so the whole interface borrows from architectural drawings: a grid background, line-drawn building illustrations, sheet numbers and dimension markers.

The site presents the company’s six service areas, three featured projects and a four-phase project process (Consultation, Design & Planning, Construction, Walkthrough), with clear quote and consultation calls to action throughout. The page itself states that it is a concept and not the company’s official website.

This is a self-initiated concept, not a paid client engagement. The live page identifies itself as a concept and not the official company website. Business details shown inside it are sample content.

The story

Why it looks and works this way.

The brief

Construction clients are making a big decision and want proof of process before they pick up the phone. We explored a site that makes the work feel planned and precise, the way a drawing set does.

How we approached it

  1. 01

    A visual language from the trade

    Grid backgrounds, sheet numbers, dimension markers and line-drawn buildings borrow the look of architectural drawings, so the design itself signals precision.

  2. 02

    Answer the practical questions at a glance

    A quick-info bar near the top shows the location, opening hours and a click-to-call phone link.

  3. 03

    Show the process, not only the results

    Four phases (Consultation, Design & Planning, Construction, Walkthrough) unfold as you scroll, so visitors can see what working together looks like.

  4. 04

    One clear next step

    Request-a-quote and consultation buttons appear throughout, so a visitor is never more than a click from the next step.

Where it stands

A finished, live concept. The live page itself states that it is a concept and not the company’s official website. This is a self-initiated project, so there are no client results to report and we don’t claim any. What it shows is how we think and build.

What’s inside

  • Blueprint visual language: grid background, drafting-sheet labels and dimension markers
  • Line-drawn building illustrations
  • Quick-info bar with location, hours and click-to-call phone link
  • Six service areas from custom homes to renovations and architectural design
  • Featured projects section
  • Four-phase project process with scroll-driven progression
  • Request-a-quote and consultation calls to action
  • Main navigation to Services, Projects, About and Contact pages
