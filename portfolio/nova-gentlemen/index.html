Project overview

NOVA GENTLEMEN is a demo website for a men’s grooming studio in Asaba. The design leans editorial: a dark interface, a high-contrast serif headline set over a full-bleed barbering photograph, and warm gold accents that give the brand a premium, tailored feel.

Beyond presentation, the site is built to turn visitors into appointments. Services are listed with durations and prices in Naira, each barber has a profile with a dedicated booking button, and a booking form lets a visitor choose a service, barber, date and time before sending the details to the studio on WhatsApp.

This is a self-initiated portfolio project, not a paid client engagement. Business names, prices, reviews and other details shown inside it are sample content created for presentation.

The story

Why it looks and works this way.

The brief

A barbershop’s customers want three answers fast: what does it cost, how long will it take, and who will cut my hair. We set out to answer all three clearly, then make booking take about a minute.

How we approached it

  1. 01

    A premium first impression

    A dark, editorial layout with a full-bleed barbering photograph and a high-contrast serif headline positions the studio as a place that takes grooming seriously.

  2. 02

    Prices and durations in the open

    Every service shows its price in Naira and how long it takes, in one clean list with a booking action beside each.

  3. 03

    Choose your barber

    Each barber has a profile and a book-with-this-barber action, so regulars can go straight to the person they trust.

  4. 04

    A booking that finishes where customers already are

    Visitors pick a service, barber, date and time, review a live summary, then confirm on WhatsApp. Time slots that have passed are marked, and an open-now indicator reflects the opening hours.

Where it stands

A finished, live demo with a working booking flow. This is a self-initiated project, so there are no client results to report and we don’t claim any. What it shows is how we think and build.

What’s inside

  • Full-bleed hero with a clear booking and WhatsApp call to action
  • Service menu with prices in Naira and appointment durations
  • Barber profiles with a book-with-this-barber action
  • Recent-work gallery and studio story
  • Booking form: choose service, barber, date and time, review a summary, then confirm on WhatsApp
  • Time-aware booking slots and an open-now indicator
  • Opening hours, address and directions section
  • Skip-to-content link and responsive layout
