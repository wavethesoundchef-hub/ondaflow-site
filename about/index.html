About ONDAFLOW

A modern digital solutions brand for businesses that want to move forward.

ONDAFLOW helps businesses establish a stronger online presence and use technology more effectively, through thoughtful design and practical digital solutions.

What we do

Digital solutions that move businesses forward.

We design and build websites, create brand identities and develop practical tools such as booking and enquiry workflows, WhatsApp-oriented flows and AI assistants.

Our focus is simple: help small businesses, startups, entrepreneurs and growing brands look professional, communicate clearly and work more efficiently.

We’re early in our journey, and we’d rather be honest about it. Our portfolio currently shows self-initiated demos and concepts, each built to explore how good design and sensible systems can solve real business problems. We label them clearly so you always know what you’re looking at.

How we think

The principles behind our work.

  • 01

    Thoughtful design

    Clarity comes first. Every layout, colour and line of copy should help someone understand your business and take the next step.

  • 02

    Practical technology

    We choose tools that solve a real problem and that you can actually use. Nothing is added just because it’s fashionable.

  • 03

    Clear communication

    Plain language, agreed scope and honest conversations, so you always know what’s happening and what comes next.

  • 04

    Business-focused execution

    Decisions are tied to what your business needs: credibility, easier enquiries and smoother day-to-day operations.

  • 05

    Continuous improvement

    Launch isn’t the finish line. We review, learn and refine, because good digital work keeps getting better.

Who’s behind it

Anyabuwa Iloba Jeremiah

I build websites, and the small systems behind them, for businesses that are good at what they do but badly served by how they look online.

Every project in this portfolio started the same way. I picked a business I could picture, a barbershop, a salon, a builder, and made the site I’d want them to have. Prices you can find in seconds. A booking that takes a minute. A message that lands straight in WhatsApp, because that’s where a lot of your customers already are.

ONDAFLOW is still young, and I’d rather say so than pretend otherwise. What I can promise is simple: you’ll talk to me, not a ticket system, and we’ll agree exactly what we’re building before I start.
