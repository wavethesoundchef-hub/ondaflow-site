Website Design & Development

Fast, responsive websites that present your business clearly and make it easy for customers to contact you.

Discuss a websiteSend a brief

What it involves

We plan, design and build your website around what your customers need to see and do. That includes page structure, layout, copy guidance, responsive behaviour across phones, tablets and desktops, and a clear path to enquiries or sales.

Who it suits

  • Service businesses such as salons, barbershops, contractors and consultants
  • Startups that need a credible launch site
  • Established businesses with an outdated or non-mobile-friendly website
  • Small shops ready to sell products online

Problems it can address

  • A website that looks dated or untrustworthy
  • Pages that are hard to use on a phone
  • Customers who can’t quickly find services, prices or contact details
  • No simple way for people to enquire or book

Deliverables

  • Responsive website design and build
  • Page structure and content layout
  • Contact, enquiry or WhatsApp integration
  • Basic SEO setup and metadata
  • Testing across devices
  • Deployment and handover

How an engagement works

  1. 1We discuss your business, audience and what the site needs to do.
  2. 2We agree the pages, structure and visual direction.
  3. 3We design and build, sharing progress so you can review.
  4. 4We test, refine and launch, then hand over what you need to manage it.

Every project is different. Scope and pricing are discussed once we understand what you need — message us to start the conversation.

Branding & Visual Identity

A distinctive identity — logo, colour and type — so your business looks consistent and professional everywhere.

Discuss your brandSend a brief

What it involves

We define how your business should look and feel, then turn that into a usable identity: a logo, colour palette, typography and simple guidelines that you can apply across your website, social media and printed materials.

Who it suits

  • New businesses launching without an identity
  • Established businesses whose look feels inconsistent
  • Brands preparing for a website or product launch
  • Entrepreneurs who want to look more professional

Problems it can address

  • A logo that doesn’t reflect the quality of your work
  • Inconsistent colours and fonts across channels
  • Difficulty standing out from similar businesses
  • Materials that look different every time they’re made

Deliverables

  • Logo design and usable logo files
  • Colour palette and typography system
  • Simple brand guidelines
  • Brand collateral such as social templates or stationery, as agreed

How an engagement works

  1. 1We learn about your business, audience and personality.
  2. 2We explore creative directions and share the strongest.
  3. 3We refine the chosen direction and build out the system.
  4. 4We deliver the files and guidance so you can use the brand confidently.

Every project is different. Scope and pricing are discussed once we understand what you need — message us to start the conversation.

03 / 04

Business Automation

Practical workflows that capture enquiries, handle bookings and reduce repetitive manual work.

Discuss automationSend a brief

What it involves

We look at how customers reach you today, then set up simple, reliable workflows: structured booking or enquiry forms, organised lead capture, follow-up routines, and WhatsApp-based flows where they suit your business.

Who it suits

  • Appointment-based businesses
  • Businesses that take most enquiries over WhatsApp
  • Teams losing leads in scattered messages
  • Owners spending hours on repetitive replies

Problems it can address

  • Enquiries arriving in unstructured messages
  • Missed or slow follow-ups
  • Manual back-and-forth to confirm a booking
  • No clear record of who asked for what

Deliverables

  • Mapped workflow for your process
  • Booking, enquiry or lead-capture setup
  • WhatsApp-oriented message flows where appropriate
  • Testing and a short guide on how to use it

How an engagement works

  1. 1We map how enquiries and bookings currently work.
  2. 2We propose a simple workflow and the tools it would use.
  3. 3We build and test it with realistic scenarios.
  4. 4We hand it over with guidance for day-to-day use.

Every project is different. Scope and pricing are discussed once we understand what you need — message us to start the conversation.

AI Solutions

Practical AI tools — chatbots, support assistants and workflow helpers — scoped to what your business actually needs.

Discuss AI for your businessSend a brief

What it involves

We identify where AI can genuinely help — answering common customer questions, drafting responses, sorting enquiries or supporting internal tasks — then build a focused tool around it. We’ll be upfront about what AI can and can’t reliably do for your case.

Who it suits

  • Businesses that answer the same questions repeatedly
  • Teams wanting faster first responses to customers
  • Owners exploring AI but unsure where to start
  • Businesses with a clear, repeatable process worth supporting

Problems it can address

  • Repetitive customer questions consuming your time
  • Slow response outside working hours
  • Information scattered across documents and chats
  • Uncertainty about where AI is actually useful

Deliverables

  • A scoped plan for where AI fits
  • A working chatbot, assistant or tool, as agreed
  • Testing with realistic questions and edge cases
  • Guidance on usage and limitations

How an engagement works

  1. 1We discuss your goals and the tasks you want to support.
  2. 2We scope a focused first version and its limits.
  3. 3We build and test it against real examples.
  4. 4We launch, review how it performs and refine.

Every project is different. Scope and pricing are discussed once we understand what you need — message us to start the conversation.
